"I started this business because I believed Oklahoma City homeowners deserved a painter who actually cares about the craft — not just getting the job done fast and moving on."

Steve Higgs founded Advance Pristine Painting with a simple but powerful idea: do the job the right way, every single time. After years working in the painting trade, Steve saw too many homeowners left with rushed work, hidden costs, and painters who didn't show up when they said they would.

He decided to do things differently. Advance Pristine Painting was built around transparency, craftsmanship, and genuine respect for the customer's home. No surprises on the bill. No cutting corners on prep. No leaving until the customer is truly happy.

Today, Steve leads a small, tight-knit team that serves Oklahoma City and surrounding areas including Edmond, Yukon, Midwest City, Norman, and Moore. Every job — from a single accent wall to a full commercial repaint — gets the same level of care and attention to detail.

When he's not painting, you'll find Steve right here in Oklahoma City, living in the same community he serves every day.
