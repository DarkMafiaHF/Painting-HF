Exterior Painting

Oklahoma's weather is no joke — intense heat, wind, and moisture can take a toll on exterior paint. We use Sherwin-Williams products specifically chosen to withstand the elements and maintain their color for years.

What's Included
  • Pressure washing and surface cleaning
  • Scraping and sanding of peeling areas
  • Caulking around windows, doors, and trim
  • Full priming before topcoat
  • Sherwin-Williams Duration or SuperPaint exterior
  • Protective drop cloths on landscaping
