💰 Pricing & Estimates
How much does interior painting cost in Oklahoma City? ▾
Interior painting in Oklahoma City typically runs $2–$4 per square foot depending on room count, ceiling height, paint quality, and wall condition. Most single-room jobs run $350–$700. A full interior (3–4 bedrooms) usually lands between $1,800–$4,500. We provide a free, detailed written estimate — no hidden fees, ever. Schedule yours here →
How much does exterior painting cost? ▾
Exterior painting costs depend on the size of your home, number of stories, siding type, and amount of prep needed. Most OKC homes run $2,500–$6,500. We include pressure washing, surface prep, priming, and two coats in every exterior quote. Call us at 405-900-1000 for a custom estimate.
Is the estimate really free? No obligation? ▾
100% free, 100% no obligation. We come to your property, assess the project, and send you a detailed written quote — usually within 24 hours. You decide from there. No pressure, no follow-up sales tactics.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
We accept cash, check, and major credit cards. We require a 50% deposit upfront to schedule and purchase materials, with the remaining 50% due upon project completion — after your final walkthrough and approval.
🎨 The Painting Process
How long does interior painting take? ▾
A single room typically takes 1–2 days. A full interior (3–4 bedrooms) usually runs 3–5 days. Commercial and larger projects are scoped individually. We give you a clear schedule before we start and stick to it.
How long does exterior painting take? ▾
Most exterior projects take 2–4 days, though larger homes or those needing extensive prep may take longer. Weather in Oklahoma City can be unpredictable — we monitor forecasts and adjust scheduling if needed to ensure proper paint adhesion and curing.
Do I need to be home during painting? ▾
Not necessarily. Many customers go about their day while we work. We just ask that you're available for the initial walkthrough at the start and the final inspection at the end so we can address anything on the spot.
How many coats of paint do you apply? ▾
We always apply at least two coats after priming, unless the surface condition makes one coat sufficient for touch-ups. Primer + two topcoats is our standard process for all full paint jobs — it's the only way to get even, lasting color.
🖌️ Paints & Products
What paint brands do you use? ▾
We use exclusively Sherwin-Williams premium products — specifically the Emerald, Duration, and SuperPaint lines. These are chosen for their superior coverage, durability, and performance in Oklahoma's climate. No cheaper alternatives, ever.
Can I supply my own paint? ▾
We prefer to supply the paint ourselves so we can guarantee the quality and stand behind the results. If you have a specific color already purchased, we'll discuss it during your estimate — we may need to supplement with our own primer or additional product.
How do I choose the right color? ▾
We bring Sherwin-Williams color swatches to your estimate so you can see colors in your actual lighting. You can also try our free to preview colors on your home before committing. Our team is happy to make recommendations based on your style and the surrounding colors.
🧹 Prep & Cleanup
Do I need to move my furniture? ▾
No — we handle everything. Our team moves and protects all furniture with drop cloths and plastic sheeting. We tape all trim, outlets, and fixtures. When we leave, your home is back to normal — just with beautiful new walls.
Do you fix wall damage before painting? ▾
Yes. Minor patching — nail holes, small dings, hairline cracks — is included in all interior painting jobs. Larger drywall repairs may be quoted separately depending on the extent of damage. We always assess this during your free estimate.
How do you protect my floors and belongings? ▾
We use heavy-duty canvas drop cloths on all floors and plastic sheeting over furniture. All trim and fixtures are taped. We treat your home like it's our own — and we clean up completely before leaving.
🏠 Services We Offer
Do you paint kitchen cabinets? ▾
Yes — cabinet refinishing is one of our most popular services. We remove all doors and hardware, clean and sand all surfaces, apply a bonding primer, spray paint for a factory-smooth finish, and reinstall everything. The result looks like brand-new cabinets at a fraction of the cost.
Do you do commercial painting? ▾
Absolutely. We work with offices, retail stores, restaurants, and multi-family residential properties. We offer flexible scheduling including nights and weekends to minimize disruption to your business operations.
What areas do you serve? ▾
